## Q1: [New Company Law] What compliance requirements apply to cross-border e-commerce platforms?

**A**: Cross-border e-commerce compliance: 1) Customs registration and e-commerce enterprise filing; 2) Products must meet Chinese quality standards; 3) Tax compliance including customs duty, VAT, and consumption tax; 4) Consumer protection including 7-day return policy; 5) Data compliance under Data Security Law and PIPL.

**法律依据**: 《海关法》

**领域**: 跨境电商 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Criminal Defense] For Expatriates: How to apply for disability assessment after a traffic accident?

**A**: Traffic accident disability assessment process: 1) Apply to qualified judicial appraisal institution after treatment completion; 2) Submit accident report, medical records, imaging materials; 3) Institution issues assessment within 30 working days; 4) Dissatisfied parties can request re-assessment.

**法律依据**: 《最高人民法院关于审理道路交通事故损害赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》

**领域**: 交通事故 | **语言**: English
